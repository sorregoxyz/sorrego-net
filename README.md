# sorrego.net

**Santiago Orrego — Experimental Ethnography**

This is the source for [sorrego.net](https://sorrego.net), a studio of
experimental ethnography. The site curates fragments — fieldnotes,
illustrations, photographs, and small taxonomies — to explore urban
infrastructures and more-than-human encounters.

Instead of polished, complete pieces, this website stores and
assembles fragments, residues, traces, and other details that reveal
how more-than-human worlds interconnect.

## What's here

- **Things I've Done** — ongoing projects and experiments, organized
  by theme: multimodal experiments, more-than-human ethnography, and
  urban infrastructure.
- **Cabinet of Definitions** — a living glossary of concepts, methods,
  and terms that accompany this work, gathered like specimens in a
  drawer rather than fixed dictionary entries.
- **Ethnographic Archive** — the same body of work, viewed instead as
  a simple timeline, newest first.

## About

I am an ethnographer working in anthropology, STS, multimodal
research, and urban studies. My work focuses on experimental
ethnography, seen as both a method and a creative practice.

I enjoy exploring urban infrastructures, more-than-human interactions,
new methods in anthropology, and the small, frequently disregarded
elements that shape everyday encounters in urban environments.

Instead of focusing on complete accounts, I pay close attention to
fragments. Fragments include residues, traces, and minimal details
that show how life is interconnected across human and nonhuman
worlds.
